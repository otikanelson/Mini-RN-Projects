A Duolingo-inspired clone, built to learn

The free, fun, and effective way to master flashcards!

DuoRecall borrows Duolingo's playful visual language to teach spaced‑repetition flashcards — built end-to-end by a solo developer as a portfolio piece.

Why DuoRecall

free. fun. effective.

Learning with DuoRecall is fun, and research shows that it works! With quick, bite-sized quiz sessions, you'll earn progress and unlock new levels while building real knowledge that sticks.

The method

backed by science

We use a combination of research-backed spaced repetition and active recall techniques to create decks that effectively teach and reinforce what you need to know.

Habit building

stay motivated

We make it easy to form a habit of learning with game-like features, fun challenges, and reminders from our friendly mascot, Duo the owl.

Made for you

personalized learning

Build your own flashcard decks on any topic. DuoRecall adapts to your pace, helping you focus on what you don't know yet so every session counts.

learn anytime, anywhere

DuoRecall fits into your life. Whether you have 5 minutes on the bus or an hour at your desk, your decks are always with you — on iOS and Android.
